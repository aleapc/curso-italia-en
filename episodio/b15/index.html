Greeting is the entry fee

In a small Italian shop the buongiorno is not optional politeness, it is how you register as a person rather than as traffic. Do solo un'occhiata then buys you the right to browse in peace.

Buongiorno, do solo un'occhiata. — Good morning, I'm just having a look.
Ci penso, grazie mille. — I'll think about it, thanks very much.

Returns are not automatic

Italian law guarantees a remedy for faulty goods, but a shop is not obliged to take back something you simply changed your mind about. Many will, within a window and with the receipt — which is exactly why you ask before you pay.

Ci penso is a complete exit

It means I'll think about it, and it is understood as a polite no without being one. Nobody expects an explanation for why you did not buy something.

Distance selling is different

The fourteen-day right of withdrawal that applies to online purchases in the EU does not extend to something you bought in person. In-store, the rule is whatever the shop told you, which is why you make them tell you.
